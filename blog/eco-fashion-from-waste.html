How to Make Eco-Friendly Fashion from Plastic Waste in India

Eco fashion from waste is becoming popular in India. EchoTrash helps you turn plastic waste into stylish and sustainable fashion products.

Step 1: Collect Plastic

Use clean plastic bottles and materials.

Step 2: Cut and Prepare

Cut plastic into usable strips.

Step 3: Create Designs

Use weaving or fusion techniques.

Step 4: Finish Product

Shape and polish your final product.

What You Can Make

Bags, belts, accessories, and eco-friendly fashion items.
