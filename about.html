About EchoTrash – Transforming Plastic Waste into Value

EchoTrash is a sustainability initiative focused on converting plastic waste into eco-friendly products while empowering youth and communities across India.

EchoTrash works at the intersection of environmental sustainability and innovation. Our mission is to reduce plastic pollution by transforming waste into useful, affordable, and eco-friendly products while creating opportunities for youth.

Founded in India

EchoTrash began as a local initiative addressing plastic waste in urban communities. It has grown into a movement promoting recycling, sustainability, and eco innovation.

Youth Empowerment

We train students and volunteers in recycling techniques, product design, and sustainable business practices, enabling them to build real-world skills.

Our Vision

Our goal is to scale sustainable solutions across India and globally, making eco-friendly living accessible and impactful.
