Our Mission: Transforming Waste into Value

EchoChic empowers Indian youth to turn plastic waste into sustainable home decor, creating economic opportunities and environmental impact.

Founded in India

Started in urban neighborhoods facing plastic pollution, EchoChic began as community cleanups and grew into a youth-led upcycling movement.

Youth Training

Our skill labs teach sorting, design, crafting, and commerce – turning volunteers into eco-entrepreneurs with real market skills.

Global Vision

From local cleanups to export-ready catalogs, we're building a sustainable model that scales across India and beyond.
