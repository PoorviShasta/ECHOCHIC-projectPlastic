Mission

Process

How We Make Upcycled Plastic Products & Eco-Friendly Home Decor

01

Collect & sort

Community cleanups, partner drives, and local drop points feed a safe, tracked plastic collection flow.

02

Clean & craft

Materials are washed, shredded, and crafted into patterns, panels, and modular home decor components.

03

Design & ship

Teams assemble products, quality-check each piece, and prepare for local sales and export showcases across India.
